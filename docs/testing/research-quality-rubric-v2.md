# Revised independent research-quality acceptance rubric

Version `research-quality-v2`, frozen 2026-10-18T00:43:33.971222+00:00. The user rejected the prior report as shallow and overreliant on the subject's own account. The required outcome is a genuine investigation yielding a coherent evidence-grounded explanation. Accurate attribution and a list of controversy topics are necessary ingredients, not sufficient research quality.

The shared design answer is used only as conceptual inspiration: maintain an evolving understanding of claims, evidence, contradictions, relationships and open questions; let the model choose the next useful inquiry; expose traceable public inferences and separate mechanical integrity from semantic adequacy. It is not treated as verified information about private OpenAI internals, and it does not prescribe a particular agent topology or ontology schema.

## Q1. Investigate actual conduct and implementation

Explain salient actions, decisions, actors/roles, mechanisms and observed implementation through concrete, evidence-grounded examples. Distinguish announcements, intentions, commitments, delivery and effects. Select examples for relevance to the question, not just ease of finding a source.

Failure pattern: Replace investigation with a portfolio list or a sequence of what organizations say they do, followed by caveats. A genuine source-backed activity description is useful background but is not the whole investigation.

Observable acceptance: The reader can explain how a consequential activity actually worked, what evidence establishes it, and what remains uncertain. The retained evidence supports the mechanism, not just a mission statement.

## Q2. Trace material funding, authority and influence

Follow salient resource and decision paths far enough to explain who supplies resources, who controls allocation, what intermediaries or restrictions matter, and how influence is exercised. Use appropriately scoped records and corroboration. Distinguish assets, pledges, commitments, recommendations and disbursements and avoid double counting. A bounded verified path can suffice; no complete movement-wide audit is required.

Failure pattern: Give a headline amount and a named funder but leave the actors, control arrangements, allocation mechanism and consequential relationships substantially unexplored. Treat a donor/organization account as the final authority on its own influence.

Observable acceptance: The report contains an intelligible evidence-based account of a salient funding/authority relationship and its implications, rather than only a statement that exact totals or control are unknown.

## Q3. Analyze interested framing and source dependencies

Identify whose perspective a source represents and its claim-specific authority, relevant interests, observation method and evidence chain. An affiliated organization, quoted response, funded study, repeated announcement or recycled dataset does not become independent merely through another URL. Test subject-defined categories and evaluative language instead of adopting them as the investigation's organizing truth.

Failure pattern: Use the subject's definition, priorities, labels, results and defense to frame the central account, relying on attribution alone to cure the dependency. Treat source counts, genre labels, publication prestige or a government-hosted subject filing as proof of independence.

Observable acceptance: The auditor can distinguish stated positions, subject-supplied records, externally observed facts and dependent interpretations, and see how those distinctions change the report's conclusions. A filing can be strong evidence within its accounting/legal remit while remaining subject-supplied. Funding ties are examined, not treated as automatic disqualification.

## Q4. Test consequential claims and competing accounts

Seek claim-relevant corroboration or a direct authoritative observation/record; inspect material contrary evidence and genuinely competing explanations. Explain what the evidence corroborates, narrows or fails to establish. One sufficiently authoritative observation can be stronger than many dependent accounts; two sources are not mechanically required.

Failure pattern: Stop at “X reports Y; not independently verified” for a central, practicably investigable claim, or regard an organization's response as verification of its conduct. Use broad philosophical disagreement as a substitute for testing answerable empirical claims.

Observable acceptance: For important assertions, the evidence account states why the claim is accepted, narrowed or unresolved and what independent observation or record bears on it. Consequential uncertainty produces an investigative action or a justified, evidenced limitation.

## Q5. Investigate outcomes and causal contribution

Separate generic intervention efficacy, actual program implementation, organizational contribution and wider effects, then investigate the task-relevant connections where feasible. Examine relevant outcome measures, applicability, selection, counterfactuals, modeling assumptions and tradeoffs. If ultimate outcomes are not observable, investigate intermediate outputs, uptake, decisions and constraints without calling them proven ultimate impact.

Failure pattern: Use generic efficacy evidence as a stand-in for investigating the subject's programs, or merely state that movement-wide attribution cannot be established and stop. Describe a pledge or spending figure as sufficient understanding of achievements.

Observable acceptance: The reader learns something grounded about actual consequences or the evidentiary reasons they remain uncertain. An impossible aggregate net-impact estimate is not required; answerable narrower contribution questions are not abandoned because the aggregate is hard.

## Q6. Explain controversies, mechanisms and accountability

Explain the substantive issue, relevant actors and responsibilities, chronology/decisions, evidence for competing accounts, responses and what followed. Distinguish allegations, corroborated reporting, formal findings, normative objections and causal conclusions. Investigate institutional mechanisms and outcomes where they bear on the question; no court judgment is required to discuss well-supported allegations.

Failure pattern: Treat naming a controversy, mentioning interview counts and adding a subject response as adequate investigative depth. Let discussion of a person substitute for institutional inquiry, or generalize wrongdoing to an entire movement.

Observable acceptance: A reader understands what the case reveals and does not reveal about conduct, incentives, power or accountability. Responses and reforms are compared with observed evidence rather than serving as the automatic endpoint.

## Q7. Deliver a coherent, earned explanation

Integrate evidence into an intelligible explanation of how the subject operates and why important findings follow. Connect claims, observations, relevant relationships, assumptions and consequences. Present the strongest earned judgments and consequential uncertainties clearly; distinguish the author's inference from source assertions.

Failure pattern: Produce an annotated source catalog or a stack of caveats and author opinions that leaves the reader to perform the central research. Accurate citations and topic mentions do not compensate for absent explanation.

Observable acceptance: The final artifact answers the core reader questions with evidence-grounded reasoning and useful specificity. An appendix can carry detail, but the main explanation must communicate the important results. Extra length is acceptable when genuinely needed; neither verbosity nor a target word count proves depth.

## Q8. Use an adaptive inquiry model and sufficient stopping rule

Maintain a revisable understanding of core questions, claims, relevant actors/relationships, supporting and contrary evidence, assumptions, consequential uncertainties and what could change conclusions. Let the model choose useful next actions to discriminate accounts or reduce material uncertainty. Update the understanding when evidence warrants it. Stop when the promised answer is supported at the requested scope and remaining uncertainty is genuinely bounded; defer optional enrichment.

Failure pattern: Run a flat search/summarize/admit/cite conveyor, or declare completion because all source items were processed. Classify central independent checks as optional merely because the self-report can be attributed. Conversely, endlessly reopen because another reachable source exists. A node/edge count, new schema or public chain-of-thought transcript is not a requirement or proof of reasoning.

Observable acceptance: A public rationale and retained evidence trajectory show why material actions were selected, what changed or remained unresolved, and why the resulting explanation is adequate. This is implementation-neutral: no mandated agent topology, ontology schema, tool ritual, citation quota or arbitrary round limit.

## Q9. Preserve epistemic fairness and accurate claim boundaries

Apply the same scrutiny to praise, criticism and defenses; report favorable and unfavorable evidence according to its strength. Preserve time, population, denominator, authority, method, uncertainty and allegation boundaries. Allow mixed or positive conclusions where earned and do not manufacture equal-weight disagreement.

Failure pattern: Force a negative verdict, assume motive from association, dismiss interested sources wholesale, convert allegations into adjudicated guilt, or substitute balanced tone for critically examining whose account has authority.

Observable acceptance: The report could survive a change in the direction of evidence: the method and standards remain the same. No predetermined conclusion or arbitrary citation/source/case ratio is used.

## Judgment and stopping

Core reader questions are answered by genuine, critically sourced investigation and an earned explanation; every applicable essential criterion is satisfied. Material uncertainties have been investigated and their remaining limits justified.

Substantive useful investigation exists but a central dimension remains inadequately established or communicated; label the limitation and do not claim full quality acceptance.

A material central dimension is replaced by interested self-description, untested consequential assertions, generic efficacy, a controversy catalog or caveat-only closure; or a serious evidentiary/framing error remains.

Prioritize unresolved issues by their importance to the promised explanation and by how an obtainable observation could change it. A central answerable gap is not optional just because a caveat is possible; a merely additional useful source is not a blocker. Genuine access/data limits are reported without invented certainty.

Independence is claim-specific, not a website label. Subject statements are appropriate evidence of stated beliefs and claims, but do not by themselves validate behavior, success, reform or exoneration. A legal filing remains subject-supplied, though its legal/accounting obligations and audit trail can give it greater authority than a marketing statement. A funded study is assessed through its methods and relevant relationships rather than automatically accepted or discarded. An external authoritative observation can sometimes establish a fact without a second source; many copies of an announcement cannot.

No citation quota, source ratio, mandatory case count or predetermined negative verdict applies. A concise report can be deep through careful selection and explanation. A long report can remain shallow. Ultimate aggregate impact may be unknowable while narrower material conduct, contribution or influence questions are answerable. Do not turn either uncertainty or an output word count into an excuse to skip those questions.

Mechanical checks retain their own PASS/FAIL result. Exact quotations, digest-bound approval, persistence and functioning UI establish integrity and operation; they do not establish investigative adequacy. Read the actual report and its substantive evidence trajectory before grading quality.

Generic criteria may guide implementation. Keep the topic-specific old-report assessment and its sources private to this independent oracle; do not send them to future research jobs. Freeze this rubric before rerunning and do not loosen it to obtain a pass.
