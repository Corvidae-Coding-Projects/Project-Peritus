- Settlement proofs cover the pure checkpoint/terminal reducer. They do not prove daemon transactions, persistence atomicity, wire authenticity, or full product-run liveness.
- Product and agent atomicity proofs cover their named in-memory kernels and caller routing. They do not prove provider truthfulness, host-resource observation, durable persistence, or complete product-run budget enforcement.
