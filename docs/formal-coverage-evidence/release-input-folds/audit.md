assessment and finding/waiver assessment to exact reductions over the supplied observations.

report/verdict conflict detection and saturated counts. Review reductions retain producer
separation, quorum, currentness, duplicate reviewer, shared context and conflicting review checks.

finding lookup and independent approved-waiver lookup. The executable equality helpers compare
every stored identity, binding, digest and discriminant field used by those checks.

The raw stored Ready verdict and public readiness predicate are equivalent to complete stored
criterion/artifact assessments, the exact supplied qualification/review/finding reductions, and
empty stored diagnostics. Ready still implies the previously proved 44 supplied artifact
contributors and complete candidate identity. This is deliberately not described as full H4
Ready iff a declarative all-input policy: criterion/artifact assessment equivalence, diagnostics
completeness and constructor admission contracts remain feasible followup work. The finite
qualification and review fold specifications also need a direct connection to declarative
quorum and all-observation requirements before those broader policy claims can be discharged.
