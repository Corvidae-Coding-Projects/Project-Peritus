The first exact-head hosted Browser workspace attempt passed 19 cases, then failed before the gateway-restart body because the expected selected-session element did not appear within the existing five-second assertion window; five later serial cases did not run. The workflow retained no failure DOM or trace artifact, and inspection established no source root cause. No source, timeout, or retry policy changed. This remains an open nonblocking qualification-reliability finding: the successful repeats do not erase the first failure or prove deterministic browser startup.
